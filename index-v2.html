How to read this: each timeframe's confluence score weighs seven inputs — trend alignment (EMA20/50/200 stack + swing structure), proximity to a key Fibonacci retracement, a detected price-action pattern, RSI momentum, MACD histogram/crossover, position within the Bollinger Bands, and volume confirmation. A score above ~65 means most of those lined up; it is not a probability of profit.

Overall Bias: the card above the panels combines all three timeframes into one weighted read (1D counts most, 15m least) plus a vote count — it's context for reading the panels, not a replacement for them.

Stop-loss logic: placed beyond the nearest structural swing point, padded by 1.5× ATR(14), so it sits outside normal noise rather than at a round number.

On leverage: this tool does not pick a leverage multiple for you. It shows the stop distance as a % of price — size your position (and any leverage) so a stop-out costs a % of your account you already decided you're fine with.

Data from Binance public market data. This is a technical-analysis tool, not financial advice — indicators describe the past, not guarantee the future. Leveraged positions can be liquidated for more than your margin. Trade size accordingly.
